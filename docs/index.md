# Welcome
### High Stakes Switzerland Validator & Staking Hub
##### App user guide, IBEX Loyalty Program, delegation guides, and FAQs

- **Swiss-hosted enterprise infrastructure** so you can stake safely and securely across Cosmos IBC networks
- **Non-custodial Staking Hub (<a href="https://app.highstakes.ch" target="_blank">app.highstakes.ch</a>)** to delegate, manage your portfolio, batch-claim rewards, auto-compound with REStake, and swap across chains
- **IBEX Loyalty Program** rewarding High Stakes delegators with daily bonus yield redeemable in **$ATOM** or **$USDC**
- **Downtime Slashing Protection** backed by publicly auditable, self-funded on-chain escrow reserves

## What You Can Do on `app.highstakes.ch`

Following the latest platform upgrade, the High Stakes web application brings your entire staking workflow into a single interface:

1. **Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: Browse all **12 supported Cosmos networks** (including **9 IBEX-eligible chains**), filter by IBEX eligibility or high yield, inspect live APRs and market prices, verify on-chain downtime slashing escrow reserves, and open the inline **Stake Management Workstation** directly on any network card.
2. **Portfolio (`/overview`)**: Track your **Total Value Staked**, **Available / Unstaked** liquid balances, **Claimable Rewards**, **Active Unbonding** schedules, **Liquid Staking Module (LSM)** positions, and **Weighted Net APY** across all chains, or delegate, redelegate, unbond, and cancel unbonding inline.
3. **Rewards (`/rewards`)**: Batch-claim all pending staking rewards across chains with **Claim All**, redeem accrued **IBEX** points for either **$ATOM** or **$USDC** (starting from 50 IBEX), and audit your historical disbursements in the **IBEX Redemption History** ledger.
4. **Cross-Chain Swap (`/swap`)**: Swap tokens seamlessly across Cosmos chains directly inside the app via **Atom Circuit Swap** (powered by Osmosis DEX liquidity).
5. **Demo Mode**: Click **Demo** in the top navigation bar (or inside the **Connect** modal) at any time to explore the full application in read-only mode without connecting a wallet.

:::caution "Security disclaimer"
Always verify the website URL (`https://app.highstakes.ch` or `https://highstakes.ch`) before signing any message or transaction with your wallet. Fake websites impersonating wallets or staking UIs circulate on the internet.

Using non-custodial wallets means you remain in sole control of your seed phrases and private keys—and are responsible for safeguarding them. High Stakes will **never** ask for your seed phrase.
