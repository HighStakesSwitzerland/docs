# Configuring Stellee

The application has many configuration options, with more features scheduled for deployment in further updates. 
Let's discover the main ones and guide you through the first setup!

## Landing Page

At the first launch, the app will request permission to send notifications, which is its core purpose: click on "Allow". 
You will then have a short introduction explaining what the application does and its main characteristics. After that, you will land on the main interface.

* ℹ️ By default, you are subscribed to a public channel containing a couple messages that give you some insight about the application, and a link to the online documentation.
* ✅ You can subscribe to other public channels by clicking on the **Featured** button in the taskbar
     
<img src={setup_1} />

## Featured

* In this page, you can directly subscribe to some public channels that are promoted. Simply click on the orange icon 🟧.
* 💡 Clicking anywhere else opens the details of the project with a few options like the available channels, managing the subscriptions, enabling/disabling the notifications, assigning categories and setting up the auto-deletion. More on that later.
* Once subscribed, new messages will appear in the main screen.
* ⚠️ When subscribing, Stellee will download the channel's content and make it available -- if no message was relayed yet in that channel, there will be nothing.

<img src={setup_2} />

## Scanning Codes

The second item from the left in the taskbar opens the camera to scan QR codes that subscribe you to a public or private channel.

* 📷 On the first use, the app will request permission to use the camera, which you should grant.
* See the [Usage documentation](./usage.mdx) to find out how to subscribe to public and private channels.
